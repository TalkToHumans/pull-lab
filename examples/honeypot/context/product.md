# Product

## What it does

Bears log each food cache (where, what, how much). Honeypot adds it up against their den date and target weight and says each morning whether they're on track, which caches to eat first, and which ones are at risk from wolves.

## Pricing

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Den | $49/month | One den, unlimited caches, shared view for up to 12 bears |

## Activation

- **Activation event:** first cache logged within 7 days of signup.
- **Why that event:** bears who log a cache in their first week pay 31% of the time, against 3% for the rest.

## Events tracked

| Event | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Signed Up` | New den created |
| `Cache Logged` | A food cache was added |
| `Forecast Viewed` | The morning on-track view was opened |
| `Den Shared` | Another bear was invited to the den |
